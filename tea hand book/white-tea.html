🤍 White Tea Profiles

Minimally processed — delicate, subtle, and sweet

What is White Tea?

White tea is the least processed of all teas. The young buds and leaves are simply steamed and dried naturally. It retains the highest amount of antioxidants and has a delicate, subtle flavor with natural sweetness.

Popular White Tea Varieties

  • Bai Hao Yin Zhen (Silver Needle) — Only unopened buds, covered in white hairs, pale liquor, incredibly delicate and sweet
  • Bai Mu Dan (White Peony) — One bud with two leaves, fuller flavor than Silver Needle, fruity and floral
  • Shou Mei — Mature leaves, darker color, bolder flavor — nutty, honeyed, and earthy
  • Gong Mei — Similar to Shou Mei, smooth and sweet with roasted notes

How to Brew White Tea

Water Temp Steeping Time Leaf Amount
70°C – 75°C 3 – 5 minutes 1–2 tsp per cup

Tip: White tea is very delicate — never use boiling water! It will ruin the subtle flavor. Look for pale golden liquor — not dark!
