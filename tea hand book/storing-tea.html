📦 Storing Tea Properly

Keep your tea fresh and flavorful longer

The 4 Enemies of Tea

  • ☀️ Light — Sunlight breaks down flavor compounds — store in opaque containers
  • 💧 Moisture — Tea absorbs water from the air — keep it dry!
  • 🌬️ Air — Oxygen makes tea go stale — seal it tight
  • 👃 Strong Odors — Tea smells like everything nearby — keep away from spices, coffee, soap

Best Storage Containers

  • ✅ Best: Tightly sealed metal tins, ceramic jars, or glass jars kept in a dark cabinet
  • ⚠️ Okay: Original packaging if resealable — transfer to a tin once opened
  • ❌ Avoid: Plastic bags alone — they don't block odors or air well enough

Shelf Life Guide

Tea Type Best Within Notes
🍵 Green & White 6 – 12 months Most delicate — drink fresh! Keep in fridge if you can
🍃 Oolong 1 – 2 years Light oolong — drink sooner; darker can last longer
🫖 Black Tea 2 – 3 years Stays fresh longer — store well and enjoy slowly
🧱 Pu-erh Many years! Needs AIR circulation — NOT airtight! It ages and improves like wine
🌿 Herbal 1 – 2 years Flowers and herbs lose potency over time — replace annually

Special Tip for Pu-erh

Unlike other teas, pu-erh needs air circulation to age properly. Store it in a breathable paper wrapper or loose ceramic jar — NOT airtight! It will slowly transform and improve in flavor over months and years.
