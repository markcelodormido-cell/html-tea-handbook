🏺 Essential Tea Ware

The right tools make all the difference

Everyday Essentials

  • Teapot — Ceramic or porcelain is best. Avoid metal — it can affect the flavor.
  • Teacup — Thick ceramic keeps tea warm longer. Clear glass lets you admire the leaves unfolding!
  • Infuser / Strainer — Mesh balls, bamboo baskets, or built-in pot filters — keeps leaves out of your cup.
  • Kettle — Gooseneck kettles give you better control when pouring.

Traditional Gongfu Tools

  • Gaiwan — A bowl with a lid and saucer. The most versatile piece — brews every type of tea perfectly.
  • Yixing Teapot — Purple clay from China. Unglazed inside — it absorbs tea oils and improves with age. Best for one type of tea only.
  • Fairness Pitcher — Pour all your tea here first, then share equally into cups. Everyone gets the same strength!
  • Tea Needle / Pick — Breaks apart compressed pu-erh cakes without damaging the leaves.
  • Tea Tray — Catches spilled water — gongfu brewing is intentionally wet!

Care & Maintenance

  • Never use soap! Hot water only — soap residue ruins the flavor of your next cup.
  • Dry thoroughly — Bamboo and wood need to air dry to prevent mold.
  • Yixing clay — Rinse with hot water only. Over time, it develops a patina that enhances your tea.
  • Glass & Ceramic — Hot water rinse is all you need. Avoid sudden temperature changes — they may crack!
