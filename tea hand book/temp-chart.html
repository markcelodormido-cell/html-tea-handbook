📊 Temperature & Steeping Time Chart

Quick reference guide for perfect tea every time

Complete Brewing Guide

Tea Type Water Temperature Steeping Time Leaf Amount
🍵 Green Tea 75°C – 80°C 2 – 3 minutes 1 tsp per cup
🤍 White Tea 70°C – 75°C 3 – 5 minutes 1–2 tsp per cup
🍃 Oolong Tea 85°C – 95°C 2 – 4 minutes 1–2 tsp per cup
🫖 Black Tea 90°C – 100°C 3 – 5 minutes 1 tsp per cup
🧱 Pu-erh Tea 95°C – 100°C 20–40 sec per steep 1 tsp per cup
🌿 Herbal Infusions 95°C – 100°C 5 – 10 minutes 1–2 tsp per cup

Quick Tips

  • Too hot? Your tea will taste bitter and harsh — especially green and white teas
  • Too cold? Your tea will taste weak and flat — flavors won't release properly
  • Too long? Most teas get bitter if over-steeped — herbal infusions are the exception!
  • Not long enough? Your tea will be pale and lack depth
  • Re-steeping: Green, white, oolong, and pu-erh can be steeped multiple times — add 30 seconds for each extra infusion
