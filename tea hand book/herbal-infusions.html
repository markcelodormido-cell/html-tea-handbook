🌿 Herbal Infusions

Naturally caffeine-free — herbs, flowers, fruits, and roots

What are Herbal Infusions?

Herbal infusions are not "true tea" — they contain no Camellia sinensis leaves. Instead, they are made from steeping fresh or dried flowers, herbs, fruits, and roots in hot water. Most are naturally caffeine-free and offer a wide range of flavors and wellness benefits.

Popular Herbal Infusions

  • Chamomile — Dried chamomile flowers, apple-like sweetness, calming and relaxing — perfect before bed
  • Peppermint — Fresh mint leaves, cool and invigorating, aids digestion and soothes the stomach
  • Hibiscus — Deep red, tart and fruity, high in Vitamin C, can be enjoyed hot or iced
  • Rooibos — South African red bush, naturally sweet and nutty, rich in antioxidants — excellent with milk and honey
  • Lemon Ginger — Zesty lemon with spicy ginger, warming and soothing — great for colds and sore throats
  • Lavender — Fragrant purple flowers, floral and calming, often blended with chamomile or mint

How to Brew Herbal Infusions

Water Temp Steeping Time Tip
95°C – 100°C 5 – 10 minutes Cover while steeping — herbs take longer than tea!

Tip: Unlike tea, herbal infusions can steep longer without turning bitter. The longer you steep, the stronger the flavor gets. Cover your cup to keep the essential oils from escaping!
