☕ Caffeine in Tea Guide

How much caffeine is in your cup?

Quick Comparison

Drink Approx. Caffeine (per cup) Notes
🌿 Herbal Infusions 0 mg Naturally caffeine-free — safe any time of day
🤍 White Tea 15 – 30 mg Lowest in true teas — gentle energy
🍵 Green Tea 25 – 45 mg Also contains L-theanine — calm alertness
🍃 Oolong Tea 30 – 60 mg In between green and black
🧱 Pu-erh Tea 30 – 70 mg Varies by age and brewing
🫖 Black Tea 40 – 90 mg Highest caffeine — great morning pick-me-up
☕ Coffee 95 – 200 mg Tea has about half as much!

Factors That Change Caffeine

  • 🌱 Which part of the plant: Buds and young leaves have MORE caffeine than older, larger leaves
  • ⏱️ Steeping time: The longer you steep, the MORE caffeine comes out — first infusion has the most
  • 🌡️ Water temperature: Hotter water extracts caffeine FASTER
  • 🌍 Where it grows: Higher altitude = slower growth = often MORE caffeine

Tea vs. Coffee — What's the Difference?

Tea contains L-theanine, an amino acid that slows caffeine absorption. This means tea gives you gentler, longer-lasting energy without the jittery spike or sudden crash that coffee can cause!

When to Drink What

  • ☀️ Morning: Black tea or strong coffee — higher caffeine
  • 🕐 Afternoon: Green, white, or light oolong — gentle energy
  • 🌙 Evening: Herbal infusions — zero caffeine, completely relaxing
