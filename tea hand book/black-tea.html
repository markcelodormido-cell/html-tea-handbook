🫖 Black Tea Profiles

Fully oxidized — bold, rich, and malty

What is Black Tea?

Black tea is fully oxidized, giving it a dark reddish-brown color and bold flavor. It comes from the same plant as green tea, but the leaves are allowed to wither and oxidize completely before drying. It holds up well to milk and sugar.

Popular Black Tea Varieties

  • Assam — Bold, malty, strong breakfast tea from India
  • Darjeeling — Floral, delicate, muscatel aroma — "Champagne of teas"
  • Ceylon — Bright, bold, citrus notes — from Sri Lanka
  • English Breakfast — Rich blend, perfect with milk
  • Earl Grey — Black tea scented with bergamot oil — citrusy and fragrant
  • Keemun — Chinese black tea, wine-like, smoky, smooth

How to Brew Black Tea

Water Temp Steeping Time Leaf Amount
90°C – 100°C 3 – 5 minutes 1 teaspoon per cup

Tip: Black tea can handle boiling water! Don't steep too long or it may become bitter.
