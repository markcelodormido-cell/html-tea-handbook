🍵 Green Tea Profiles

Unoxidized tea — fresh, grassy, and delicate

What is Green Tea?

Green tea is made from the leaves of the Camellia sinensis plant that have not undergone oxidation. The leaves are heated soon after picking to stop natural oxidation, preserving their green color and fresh, vegetal flavor. Originating in China, green tea is now enjoyed worldwide.

Popular Green Tea Varieties

  • Long Jing (Dragon Well) — Pan-roasted, chestnut aroma, flat and smooth leaves
  • Sencha — Japanese steamed green tea, bright green, grassy and refreshing
  • Matcha — Finely ground green tea powder, whisked into hot water
  • Gyokuro — Shade-grown, rich and sweet, high in caffeine
  • Bi Luo Chun — Downy leaves, fruity aroma, rolled into tiny spirals
  • Jasmine Green Tea — Scented with jasmine flowers, sweet and floral

How to Brew Green Tea

Water Temp Steeping Time Leaf Amount
75°C – 80°C 2 – 3 minutes 1 teaspoon per cup

Tip: Never use boiling water — it will burn the leaves and make the tea bitter!
