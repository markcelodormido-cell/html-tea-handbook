🧱 Pu-erh Tea Basics

Aged and fermented tea — improves over time

What is Pu-erh Tea?

Pu-erh is a unique fermented tea from Yunnan, China. Unlike other teas, it is pressed into cakes or bricks and can be aged for years — like fine wine. The aging process changes its flavor, making it smoother and deeper over time.

Two Main Types

  • Raw / Sheng Pu-erh — Sun-dried, natural aging process, can be bitter when young, transforms over years — bright, bold, evolving
  • Ripe / Shou Pu-erh — Accelerated fermentation process, smooth, earthy, dark liquor, ready to drink right away — no waiting needed

How to Brew Pu-erh Tea

Water Temp Steeping Time Preparation Tip
95°C – 100°C First rinse: 10 sec
Then 20–40 sec per steep		 Rinse leaves first with hot water, then brew

Tip: Pu-erh can be steeped many times — 10+ infusions! Each cup tastes slightly different. Always "wake up" the leaves with a quick rinse first.
