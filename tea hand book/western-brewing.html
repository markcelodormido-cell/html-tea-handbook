☕ Western Style Brewing

Simple, one-pot method — perfect for daily enjoyment

What is Western Style Brewing?

This is the everyday method most people use — one steep, one cup or pot, easy and convenient. It works great for all tea types and requires no special equipment beyond a cup or teapot.

What You'll Need

  • Teacup or Teapot — Ceramic or porcelain works best
  • Tea Infuser / Strainer — To keep leaves out of your mouth
  • Hot Water — At the right temperature for your tea
  • Loose-leaf Tea — About 1 teaspoon per cup

Step-by-Step Method

  1. Warm your cup or pot — Rinse with a little hot water, then pour it out. This keeps your tea hot longer.
  2. Add tea — Place about 1 teaspoon of loose-leaf tea into your infuser or pot, per cup of water.
  3. Add hot water — Pour water at the correct temperature over the leaves. Don't use boiling water for green or white teas!
  4. Cover & wait — Put a lid or saucer on top to keep the heat in. Steep according to the chart.
  5. Remove leaves — Take out the infuser or strain the tea into another cup. Don't let leaves sit in the water — it will get bitter!
  6. Enjoy! — Add milk, honey, or lemon if you like. Sip and savor.

Quick Reference

Tea Type Water Temp Steep Time
Green 75°C – 80°C 2 – 3 minutes
White 70°C – 75°C 3 – 5 minutes
Oolong 85°C – 90°C 3 – 4 minutes
Black 90°C – 100°C 3 – 5 minutes
Herbal 95°C – 100°C 5 – 10 minutes

Tip: If you like your tea stronger, use slightly more tea leaves — don't just steep longer. Steeping too long makes tea bitter, not stronger!
