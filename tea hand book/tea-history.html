📜 The History of Tea

From ancient legend to the world's most beloved beverage

The Legendary Beginning

According to Chinese legend, tea was discovered in 2737 BCE by the Emperor Shen Nong. While resting beneath a tree, a few leaves blew into his bowl of hot water. He loved the golden infusion so much — tea was born!

Timeline Through Time

  • 🌿 2700s BCE — China: First used as medicine, then as a beverage. For centuries, only green tea existed.
  • 📖 Tang Dynasty (618–907): Tea becomes a daily drink. Lu Yu writes the first book about tea — The Classic of Tea.
  • 🎨 Song Dynasty (960–1279): Whisked powdered tea — the precursor to modern Matcha. Tea becomes an art form.
  • 🌏 800s–1200s — Spreads East: Buddhist monks bring tea to Japan — where it evolves into the Japanese tea ceremony.
  • ⚙️ Ming Dynasty (1368–1644): Loose-leaf tea is invented. People discover oxidation — and black and oolong teas are born!
  • 🚢 1600s — Reaches the West: Dutch and Portuguese traders ship tea to Europe. It becomes an expensive luxury.
  • 🇬🇧 1600s–1800s — British Love Affair: The British fall in love with tea. They plant it in India and Ceylon — making it affordable worldwide.
  • 🌎 Today: Tea is the second most drunk beverage on Earth — after water. Enjoyed in every country, in thousands of forms.

Fun Facts

  • All "true tea" — green, black, oolong, white, pu-erh — comes from the exact same plant: Camellia sinensis!
  • For thousands of years, tea was boiled, eaten, or whisked — it's only been steeped for the last 600 years.
  • Before sugar and milk became common, tea was often seasoned with salt, ginger, or even onions!
