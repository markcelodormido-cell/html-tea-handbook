💧 The Science of Water

Why water matters — it's 99% of your tea!

Why Water Matters

Tea is 99% water — so the quality of your water directly affects the taste of your tea. Even the best leaves can taste bad if brewed with poor-quality water.

What Kind of Water to Use

  • ✅ Best: Filtered or spring water — balanced minerals, clean taste
  • ⚠️ Okay: Tap water — let it run cold first; if it smells like chlorine, don't use it
  • ❌ Avoid: Distilled water — too flat, no minerals = flat-tasting tea
  • ❌ Avoid: Hard water — too many minerals = bitter, cloudy tea

Temperature Guide

Tea Type Ideal Temp Why
White & Green 70°C – 80°C Prevents burning the delicate leaves
Oolong 85°C – 95°C Unlocks deeper flavors
Black, Pu-erh & Herbal 95°C – 100°C Needs heat to release full flavor

Quick tip: If you don't have a thermometer — boil water, wait 1 minute for green/white tea, then pour!
